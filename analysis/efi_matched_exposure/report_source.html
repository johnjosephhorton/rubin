Specification audit · Prediction #2 · 872 O*NET occupations

The Fragmentation Null

Measuring AI exposure and the fragmentation index on the same set of steps removes the fragmentation effect from the main text and from two of three robustness appendices. The coefficient was the exposure level in disguise.

The empirical fragmentation index counts a step as AI-able when its Eloundou label is E1 or E2. The published regressions control for the E1 share alone. That gap is the whole finding.

EFI  =  (m − k + r) / m  =  1  − k/m  + r/m

For a workflow of m steps with k AI-able steps forming r maximal runs: an exposure level term entering with coefficient exactly −1, plus an arrangement term. Verified to exact float equality (max deviation 0.000e+00) on all 28 estimation samples in the paper.

Because the E1∪E2 level inside the index is never absorbed, the EFI coefficient loads on −k/m rather than on r/m. It measures exposure, not arrangement. The correlation between the EFI and the E1∪E2 share is −0.954, and 91% of the index's variance is that share; the E1 share the paper actually controls for absorbs only 50% of it.

02 · PrecisionWhat the matched design can and cannot rule out

Both halves of the power claim hold, column by column. The minimum detectable effect at 80% power and a 5% two-sided test is 0.282 / 0.240 / 0.261 in standard-deviation units, which is 4.47 / 3.80 / 4.13 percentage points. A 4,000-draw wild residual bootstrap reproduces those thresholds at 0.284 / 0.239 / 0.247 with correct size, so 2.802 × SE is the right number to quote.

The honest two-sided statement

The design is underpowered for a modest effect: anything up to roughly a 3-point true reduction would routinely go undetected. But it does exclude the published magnitude in all three columns, with two-sided p of 0.012 / 0.001 / 0.009 treating the published point as fixed.

The exclusion is comfortable under major-group fixed effects (gap 0.126 SD) and marginal in the other two columns (gaps 0.057 and 0.061, both under one percentage point). Worth stating as marginal rather than as a clean rejection.

The cost of the fix is a 1.7× to 1.9× variance inflation on the EFI coefficient. Once the E1∪E2 level and the step count are absorbed, only 30.0% of the index's standard deviation survives under no fixed effects, falling to 23.4% under minor-group FE. That residual is the entire identifying variation the arrangement channel has left, and it is what the confidence intervals are reporting.

03 · AppendicesThe robustness results do not move together

Two appendices lose the result and turn into stable nulls. One survives, for a reason that is visible in the data. One was never evidence.

SA.D · Eleven GPT prompt orderings

Under the published specification all 33 prompt-by-column cells were negative and significant. Under the matched specification none reaches 5% and only two reach 10%. The important point for the appendix is that this is not prompt fragility: the between-prompt dispersion of the matched coefficient (SD 0.044 / 0.023 / 0.040) is two to four times smaller than the sampling noise in any single estimate, and all 33 estimates sit inside the main-prompt confidence interval.

Why APQC survives and O*NET does not

Not a difference in ordering source. The 0.73 similarity floor leaves the PCF corpus at an 8.6% E1∪E2 density against 43.2% in O*NET, so the index's level term no longer dominates it. The R² of the EFI on the exposure share is 0.58 there against 0.91 in the main sample, and 65% of the index's standard deviation survives conditioning on exposure against 30% in O*NET. Putting E1∪E2 on the right-hand side removes far less identifying variation, and the VIF is 2.4 rather than 11.

The observed coefficient still sits outside the within-group reshuffle null (z about −3.1). Note the placebo z is seed-dependent across three seeds at −3.05 to −3.19, with 0 to 3 of 1000 draws below the observed value, so "below all 1000 draws" holds for some seeds only.

This inverts the appendix's logic. SA.F currently reads its −0.37 against a main-text range of −0.26 to −0.38. Under the matched specification the external validation would be reporting a larger and better-identified fragmentation effect than the specification it is validating, and that has to be said out loud rather than papered over.

SA.B · The execution-based index was never evidence

Worse than the paper's footnote concedes. The level term of the execution-based EFI is bitwise identical to the dependent variable, max deviation 0.000e+00, so the table regresses y on (1 − y + r₂/m). Substituting that algebraic equivalent leaves the coefficients and R² unchanged to the digit.

Changing the exposure regressor does not repair it: matched, the coefficient barely moves, from −0.780 / −0.703 / −0.679 to −0.777 / −0.682 / −0.666. Isolating the arrangement term alone flips the sign to +0.871 / +0.748 / +0.715. And 317 of 872 occupations, 36.4%, have zero AI-executed steps, so their index is exactly 1 and their outcome exactly 0 by construction. This is open review issue D31, now with a proof; it should be raised to Major.

04 · Rescue attemptsNo better arrangement statistic recovers the effect

The natural objection is that the EFI is simply a poor measure of arrangement. It is not, and the evidence runs the other way.

The definition grid

Crossing three exposure definitions with three EFI definitions and three fixed-effect specifications gives 27 cells. Fifteen are significantly negative at 5%, twelve of them at 1%, and every one is off-diagonal, meaning the exposure label set and the EFI label set disagree. Every matched cell is null or wrong-signed. The E2-on-E2 cell is significantly positive without fixed effects.

Correcting one thing I told you earlier

The −0.211 in the repo's own exposure_definition_grid.csv is not about the step-count control. Dropping the control moves the matched coefficient only from −0.007 to −0.019, both null. The −0.211 comes from the 871-occupation sample, which drops the 789 task rows with missing human_labels and recomputes m.

The mechanism: 183 of the 2,998 maximal runs in the 872 panel, 6.1%, exist only because an unlabelled task sitting inside a block of E1∪E2 tasks is coded "not AI-able" and splits the block.

On the 871 sample the matched EFI is −0.209 (p = 0.031) with no FE and −0.192 (p = 0.025) under major-group FE. Reaching that takes three stacked choices, and dropping any one kills it: drop the unlabelled rows, keep the 62 zero-exposure occupations whose EFI is 1.0 by construction (restricting to k ≥ 1 gives −0.173, p = 0.106), and avoid minor-group fixed effects (−0.105, p = 0.291). On that same 871 sample all four scale-free statistics are indistinguishable from zero. A census of 192 matched specifications finds 21 significant with the predicted sign, all 21 on the 871 sample and all 21 the same statistic.

Method. Ten agents: four re-estimating an appendix each, three running diagnostics, three verifying independently. Every estimate was reproduced twice by scripts written without reference to each other, and each script regenerates the published numbers before changing anything. Both numeric verifiers returned CONFIRMED with no disagreement in any coefficient.

Reproducing. Scripts are in analysis/efi_matched_exposure/ with a README. All 18 run clean and take repo-relative paths.
