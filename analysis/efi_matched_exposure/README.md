# EFI under a matched exposure definition

Re-estimation of every empirical-fragmentation-index (EFI) result in the paper after matching
the exposure regressor to the set of steps the EFI is built from.

**Nothing here modifies the draft.** Every published table and figure in `writeup/` is
untouched. These scripts reimplement the construction of the notebook each one shadows,
reproduce the published numbers first, then change one variable.

## The problem

EFI Definition 1 counts a step as AI-able when its Eloundou label is E1 **or** E2, and merges
consecutive AI-able steps. Per occupation:

| | |
|---|---|
| `m` | number of steps in the workflow |
| `k` | how many of them are AI-able, the *amount* of AI-able material |
| `r` | how many separate **blocks** those AI-able steps form, the *arrangement* of it |

A block is a group of consecutive AI-able steps that cannot be extended, so an isolated AI-able
step is a block of size 1 and `r` counts blocks rather than their lengths. Each block is one
potential AI chain. `r` runs from 1 when the AI-able steps are all contiguous to `k` when no two
are adjacent, so at fixed `k` it indexes nothing but arrangement. Then

```
EFI = (m - k + r)/m = 1 - k/m + r/m
```

an exposure **level** term entering with coefficient exactly `-1`, plus an **arrangement**
term. The published regressions control for the **E1 share alone**, so the E1∪E2 level inside
the index is never absorbed and the EFI coefficient loads on `-k/m` rather than on `r/m`.

The matched specification puts the E1∪E2 share on the right-hand side. The `num_E1E2_tasks`
step-count control is kept; that is the headline throughout, per the decision of 2026-09-01.

## Results

### Main text, Table 2 (N = 872)

Standardized coefficients, SE clustered on O\*NET-SOC code. `table2_and_SAB.py` regenerates
both published `.tex` files byte-for-byte before changing anything.

| | (1) no FE | (2) Major FE | (3) Minor FE |
|---|---|---|---|
| EFI, published | -0.261 (0.073)\*\*\* | -0.380 (0.064)\*\*\* | -0.283 (0.071)\*\*\* |
| **EFI, matched** | **-0.007 (0.101)** | **-0.086 (0.086)** | **-0.040 (0.093)** |
| matched 95% CI | [-0.204, +0.191] | [-0.254, +0.082] | [-0.222, +0.143] |
| Exposure, published (E1) | +0.241 (0.046)\*\*\* | +0.109 (0.042)\*\*\* | +0.092 (0.047)\* |
| **Exposure, matched (E1∪E2)** | **+0.494 (0.102)\*\*\*** | **+0.479 (0.094)\*\*\*** | **+0.390 (0.093)\*\*\*** |

In levels the EFI effect goes from -4.13 / -6.01 / -4.48 percentage points of the
AI-execution share per SD to -0.11 / -1.36 / -0.63.

### Where the published coefficient came from

With the EFI, the E1 share and the E1∪E2 share all entered, the EFI is
-0.010 / -0.080 / -0.044 (p = 0.92 / 0.35 / 0.63) while E1∪E2 is +0.360 / +0.438 / +0.355
(p < 0.001). An exact omitted-variable identity attributes **96.3% / 79.0% / 84.4%** of the
published EFI coefficient to the omitted E1∪E2 level.

### Power

80%-power MDE is 0.282 / 0.240 / 0.261 SD units (4.47 / 3.80 / 4.13 pp), confirmed by a
4,000-draw wild residual bootstrap. Each published estimate falls outside the matched 95% CI:
comfortably under major-group FE (gap 0.126) and marginally in the other two columns (0.057
and 0.061, both under 1 pp). Underpowered for a modest effect, but the published magnitude is
excluded.

### The appendices split three ways

| Appendix | Matched result | Claim as written |
|---|---|---|
| SA.B, execution-based EFI | -0.777 / -0.682 / -0.666, still \*\*\* | Mechanical, see below |
| SA.D, 11 prompt orderings | 0 of 33 cells at 5%, 2 at 10% | Fails; becomes a stable null |
| SA.E, 12 frequency cuts | 0 of 39 cells at 5%, either sign | Fails; becomes a stable null |
| SA.F, APQC (n = 525) | -0.295\*\* / -0.250\* / -0.290\*\* | **Survives** |

SA.D's null is stable rather than fragile: between-prompt dispersion (SD 0.02 to 0.04) is two
to four times smaller than the sampling noise in a single estimate, and all 33 matched
estimates sit inside the Prompt 0 interval. SA.E's three largest cuts give precise zeros
(SE 0.085 to 0.101). In both, the exposure coefficient strengthens: minor-FE exposure went
from significant in 0 of 11 orderings to 11 of 11.

SA.F survives for a reason visible in the data. The 0.73 similarity floor leaves the PCF
corpus at 8.6% E1∪E2 density, so the R-squared of the EFI on the exposure share is 0.58 there
against 0.91 in O\*NET and 65% of the index's variation survives conditioning, against 30% in
the main sample.

### The execution-based EFI is worse than the paper concedes

Its level term is bitwise identical to the dependent variable
(`max |k2/m - ai_fraction| = 0.000e+00`), so SA.B regresses `y` on `(1 - y + r2/m)`.
Substituting that algebraic equivalent leaves coefficients and R-squared unchanged to the
digit. Isolating the arrangement term flips the sign to **+0.871 / +0.748 / +0.715**.
Changing the exposure regressor does not repair it. See `SAB_def2_degeneracy.py`.

### No better arrangement statistic rescues it

`arrangement_statistics.py` runs a 27-cell definition grid and six alternative measures.

1. In the grid, **all 15 significantly negative cells are off-diagonal**, meaning the EFI
   label set and the exposure label set disagree. Every matched cell is null or wrong-signed.
2. The scale-free measures find nothing: `r/k` +0.025 (p = 0.59), adjacency share -0.022
   (p = 0.47), longest run over `k` -0.029 (p = 0.30), permutation z-score +0.032 (p = 0.28).
   The permutation z is four times better powered than the EFI (MDE 0.06 to 0.08 against 0.24
   to 0.28) and comes out wrong-signed.

### The -0.211 in the repo's own grid

`data/computed_objects/apqc_pcf_fragmentation/exposure_definition_grid.csv` reports the
matched O\*NET spec at -0.211 (p = 0.031). `sample_871_vs_872.py` traces it: **not** the
step-count control (dropping that moves -0.007 to -0.020), but the 871-occupation sample,
which drops the 789 task rows with missing `human_labels` and recomputes `m`. 183 of the 2,998
blocks in the 872 panel, 6.1%, exist only because an unlabelled task inside a block of
E1∪E2 tasks is coded "not AI-able" and splits the block.

Reaching the significant result takes three stacked choices, any one of which kills it: drop
the unlabelled rows; keep the 62 zero-exposure occupations whose EFI is 1.0 by construction
(`k >= 1` gives -0.173, p = 0.106); avoid minor-group FE (-0.105, p = 0.291). On that same
871 sample all four scale-free statistics are null. `discretion_census.py` runs 192 matched
cells: 21 are significant with the predicted sign, all 21 on the 871 sample and all 21 the
same statistic.


### Does the count control matter?

`step_count_controls.py` runs 2 samples x 2 exposure regressors x 5 control sets x 3 FE.
Short answer: on the matched specification, 872 sample, it does not. All 15 cells are null,
the spread within an FE column is at most 0.033 against a standard error of about 0.09, and
adding `m` (workflow length, the D29 proposal) moves the coefficient by 0.004 to 0.015. An
adversarial search over 2,229 specifications found zero FE cells significant at 5%.

Two cautions recorded in `writeup/EFI_MATCHED_SPECIFICATION.md` section 5b: the controls agree
for an empirical reason rather than an algebraic one (the R-squared of `m` on `(1, k/m, k)` is
0.659, not 1), and it is the 871-occupation result whose significance is control-dependent,
not the 872 one.

## Verification

The three `verify/` scripts are independent reimplementations of SA.D, SA.E and SA.F, written
from the construction rather than from the primary scripts, and they agreed with those scripts
on every coefficient. The remaining results (Table 2, the arrangement statistics, the MDE
bootstrap, the omitted-variable identity, the discretion census and the step-count grid) have a
single producer each and were checked against the published numbers rather than against a
second implementation.

`table2_and_SAB.py` regenerates the two O\*NET tables byte-for-byte, which is the strongest
available check that the replication is of the right object. For the APQC table the
replication matches every number but carries one extra line, a significance-footnote row that
the emitter writes and that was deleted by hand in the committed file; it is identical once
that row is dropped.
