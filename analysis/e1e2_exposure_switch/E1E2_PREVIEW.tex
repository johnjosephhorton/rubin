\begin{prose}
\textbf{What changed.} One line in the estimation code: the AI-exposure indicator went from
\file{human_labels.isin(['E1'])} to \file{human_labels.isin(['E1','E2'])}, so a task counts as
AI-exposed if the Eloundou et al.\ label is E1 \emph{or} E2 rather than E1 only. On the main
10,708-row estimation sample this takes the exposed share from $0.1422$ to $0.4326$
(1,523 exposed rows \ra{} 4,632; 3,109 rows flip $0\!\to\!1$, none flip $1\!\to\!0$).
Exposure is a right-hand-side control, never a sample filter, which is why every regression
$N$ in this document is unchanged.
\end{prose}
